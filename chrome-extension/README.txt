Save to My Job Tracker — Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)
========================================================

How to install in Chrome / Edge / Brave:
1. Extract this downloaded ZIP folder on your computer.
2. Open chrome://extensions in your browser.
3. Turn ON "Developer mode" (top-right toggle).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and select this extracted folder.
5. Pin "Save to My Job Tracker" to your browser toolbar.
6. In My Job Tracker -> Connected Portals -> Chrome Extension, click "Copy Pairing Code" and paste it inside the extension popup ("Extension Pairing & Connection Test"), then click "Save & Test Pairing".

How it works safely:
- Never collects or stores your LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, WorkIndia, or Apna passwords.
- Never automates logins, never bypasses CAPTCHAs, and never submits applications automatically.
- Captures only the visible job details on the tab you are viewing when you click "Save to My Job Tracker".
- Always asks you to confirm whether you actually applied ("Applied – Manually Recorded") or are bookmarking the job ("Saved").
